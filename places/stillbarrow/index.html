Stillbarrow

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 20-34 · Danger: Severe · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A town of the dead in the grey hills, dug into the old barrows: skeletons keeping shop, ghouls running the bone trade, golems at the gates. It's quiet, it's orderly, and the living who come here keep their voices down.

Areas

  • Bell Barrow: A barrow with a cracked bell hung over it, rung once when someone new joins the town.
  • Bone Market: A sunken square of old barrows turned into stalls, bones sorted by size, buyers speaking in whispers.
  • Bone Stair: A stair of fitted bones down into the deepest barrows, swept clean every night.
  • Coffin Row: A street of workshops where the skeletons make coffins for anyone who'll pay, measuring you up whether you asked or not.
  • Crypt Rows: Streets of open crypts where the town's dead live, lamps burning in the doorways.
  • Embalmers' House: A barrow where the newly dead are prepared and the living can pay to be ready.
  • Golem Works: A barrow hall full of half-built golems, clay and stone and iron limbs hung from the ceiling.
  • Grave Gardens: Rows of grey flowers grown over the graves of the ones who chose to stay dead.
  • Lamp Street: The town's main street of barrow doors, a lamp lit at every one and nobody shouting.
  • Mourners' Gate: The gate into town, golems standing either side, where the living are told the rules.
  • Quiet Hall: The council's hall inside the biggest barrow, cold and silent, with stone seats round a long table.
  • Waking Room: A cold stone room where the town's new dead are laid out to get up, and the oldest skeleton in town is there to tell them how it works.
