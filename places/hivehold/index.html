Hivehold

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 15-30 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

An antfolk city carved into a whole mountain: galleries, roads and halls running up inside the peak, millions of feet on the move. Outsiders are allowed in the guest ledge and the ore galleries and nowhere else.

Areas

  • Aphid Pastures: A warm gallery lit by glowing fungus where the antfolk keep herds of fat green aphids and milk them for honeydew.
  • Dead Chamber: A deep chamber where the hive carries its dead, stacked neat and dry, and the workers come back for them when the hive is hungry.
  • Fungus Farms: Long warm galleries where the hive grows its food on beds of chewed leaves.
  • Guest Ledge: A wide ledge cut into the mountainside where outsiders are met, traded with and watched.
  • Hatch Tunnels: Warm tunnels deep in the hive where the young are raised; closed to outsiders.
  • Ore Galleries: Long galleries where antfolk workers haul ore out of the mountain in endless lines.
  • Queen's Gallery: The great hall at the heart of the mountain where the queen lies; nobody from outside has seen it.
  • Soldier Galleries: Galleries where the soldiers drill in silence and every outsider is watched.
  • Trade Chamber: A round chamber off the guest ledge where the hive's traders bargain with outsiders through a grille.
  • Upper Vents: Vents near the peak where hot air from the hive pours out into the cold.
  • Waste Shafts: Deep shafts where the hive drops everything it can't use, and things live at the bottom.
  • Worker Roads: Wide roads inside the mountain packed with antfolk workers moving in lines that never break.
