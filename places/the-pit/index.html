The Pit

[Recommended Level: 45-50 · Danger: Deadly]

A great round paved yard in the middle of the prison with a ring wall round it and a deep round pit in its centre, four stairs going down into it from the four sides and a slow violet swirl at the bottom you can't see past. Whatever the prison was built for is down there. The four stairs only go halfway down, the gold rings set round the yard's floor glow brighter every night, and the trolls who sweep the yard won't go near the edge.

Areas

  • The Half Stairs: The four stairs going down into the pit that stop halfway.
  • The Ring Yard: The round paved yard round the pit, gold rings set in the floor.
  • The Swirl Below: The violet swirl at the bottom of the pit.
