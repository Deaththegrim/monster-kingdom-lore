Stiltscale

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 10-24 · Danger: High · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A lizardfolk town built on stilts over the black water, huts joined by rope walks, boats tied under every floor. It smells of fish and peat smoke and there's always something big moving under the walkways.

Areas

  • Boat Moorings: Boats tied under the town's floors, reed and hide, knocking against the stilts.
  • Chief's Lodge: The biggest hut, built on the thickest stilts, hung with old skulls and dried hydra heads.
  • Croc Pens: Fenced channels under the town where the big crocodiles are fed and kept.
  • Eel Traps: Wicker traps sunk along the channels, checked at dawn by the young ones.
  • Hatchery: A warm, steamy hut over a peat fire where the town's eggs are kept, guarded day and night.
  • Mud Baths: A ring of warm mud pools under the huts where the lizardfolk lie in the heat all afternoon and do business with their eyes shut.
  • Net Shed: A long shed on stilts hung with nets and floats, the old lizardfolk mending them and telling anyone who'll listen about the big one.
  • Peat Stacks: Platforms piled high with cut peat drying for the fires.
  • Reed Shrine: A small shrine of bundled reeds over the water, offerings of fish heads left for whatever lives below.
  • Rope Walks: Rope walkways strung between the huts on stilts, swaying over the black water.
  • Smoke Huts: Huts where fish and eel hang in peat smoke until they'll keep for a season.
  • Stilt Market: A wide platform over the water where boats pull up to sell fish, peat and reeds.
