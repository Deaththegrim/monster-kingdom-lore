Ringgrove

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 5-20 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A town grown rather than built: a ring of huge trees with homes in their trunks and branches, bridges of living wood between them, mushroom halls at their roots. Dryads, treants, myconids and fox spirits live here, and none of them like axes.

Areas

  • Bark Market: Stalls grown into the side of a trunk selling herbs, honey, sap and mushrooms.
  • Branch Bridges: Living branches grown into walkways between the great trees, high above the forest floor.
  • Dryad Pools: Clear pools between the roots where the dryads bathe and nobody stares if they want to keep their eyes.
  • Fox Shrine: A small red shrine in the hollow of a tree, always with something left on it, always taken by morning.
  • Leaf Loft: A platform high in the branches roofed with living leaves, where travellers sleep in hammocks and wake up covered in pixies.
  • Pixie Hollow: A glowing hollow full of tiny houses in the bark, buzzing and giggling, where nothing you drop stays yours.
  • Root Steps: Steps grown out of the roots down to the forest road, where the caravans tie up.
  • Sap House: A hollow in a great tree where the dryads tap the sap and sell it in cups, sweet and strong enough to knock out an orc.
  • Seed Store: A hollow trunk packed with seeds, nuts and roots against the winter, guarded by squirrels that bite.
  • Spore Hall: A great mushroom grown into a hall at the roots, lit soft blue, where the myconids share their dreams.
  • The Ring: The clearing inside the ring of trees, soft with moss, where the grove holds its gatherings.
  • Treant Circle: A ring of old treants standing so still they look like trees, where the grove's oldest law is kept.
