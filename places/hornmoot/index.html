Hornmoot

[Town Core · Recommended Level: 5-16 · Danger: Moderate · No major hostile encounters within the settlement itself; surrounding ground remains dangerous]

A trading camp in the long grass round a standing stone with horns carved in it, where the herds meet. Centaurs, minotaurs and boarfolk come in to trade hides, meat and stock, and the gnolls come in pretending they aren't there to steal.

Areas

  • Bone Carvers: A row of awnings where horn and bone are carved into tools, charms and trade tokens.
  • Calving Ground: A fenced field on the camp's edge where the herds bring their young, guarded day and night by the biggest minotaurs.
  • Elders' Fire: A fire that never goes out at the camp's heart, where each herd's eldest sits and nobody else does.
  • Herd Pens: Fenced pens of stock on the camp's edge where the caravans load and the beetles graze.
  • Hide Market: Racks of stretched hides and piles of furs, horn and hoof, the herds' trade laid out on the grass.
  • Hoof Smithy: A big open tent with an anvil and a fire where the farrier shoes centaurs and minotaurs, both holding very still and both hating it.
  • Horn Stone: The great standing stone in the middle, horns carved deep into it, polished by every hand that has sworn on it.
  • Moot Ring: A wide ring of standing stones round the horn stone, where disputes are shouted out and settled.
  • Racing Ground: A long strip of trampled grass where the centaurs race and the whole camp bets on it.
  • Smoke Tents: Low tents full of smoke where meat is cured and the old ones sit telling the same stories.
  • Tent Rows: Rows of hide tents, every herd with its own colours on the poles.
  • Trough Line: A long line of water troughs dug into the grass, every herd taking its turn and arguing about it.
