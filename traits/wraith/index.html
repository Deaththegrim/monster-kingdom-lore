Wraith

The candles gutter all at once, your breath fogs in the middle of summer and frost creeps across the walls in thin white fingers, and that's the only warning you get before a wraith comes through the stone. They're the cold shadows of souls that wouldn't move on, haunting battlefields and ruins and the dungeon's dark, and they drain the warmth out of anything living they touch until it's as cold as they are. Some still remember who they were and can be talked to, and they'll bargain hard for whatever it was that kept them here, a name, a grave, a debt nobody paid, but they never quite understand that the living can't wait forever for an answer.

🔮 Absorb +4 · ⚔️ Cold Touch · 🧱 resists slashing, piercing, bludgeoning · ⚠️ weak to holy · 🚫 immune to poison, cold

Any build.
