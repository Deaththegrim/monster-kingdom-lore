What to say

The exact lines that set off the Wildreach's systems: souls, your home and kingdom, factions, caravans and walking.

  • Take a fallen foe's soul: Right after a kill, within two turns: name the soul and take it, or eat the body. Each race's souls stack toward its gifts and, at ten, its Soul.
  • Make a place your home: Where you stand, saying you live here now. One home; everything you claim later answers to it.
  • Claim a place as yours: Where you stand, once you have a home: plant your mark and it pays into your kingdom.
  • Ask someone to live at your home: Once you have a home, with them in the scene.
  • Give a resident a post: Once someone lives at your home: name the post they keep.
  • Start a build project: At home, once the kingdom is ready to grow.
  • Stash goods at home: Once you have a home: store or take back what you carry.
  • Hit back at raiders: After your home or a holding was raided: go after the ones who did it.
  • Swear to a faction: In a scene, naming the faction, before someone with the standing to accept it.
  • Ask a faction for a job: At the faction's seat, sworn to it, asking an officer.
  • Sell one faction out to another: Sworn to two at once: betray one to the other.
  • Take a caravan: At a town's caravan yard. Name where you are going; the fare is taken in the scene.
  • Walk to a place you know: Name it: the walk lands you there instead of on the road.
  • Say how your element looks: Once you hold an element: describe it and the narrator keeps it that way.
