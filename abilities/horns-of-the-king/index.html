Horns of the King

What it does: you charge at the head of every horned ally on the field; together your charge hits hardest of all and breaks a battle line.

You lower your great crowned head and every horned thing on the field lowers its own, bulls, rams, minotaurs down to the last calf-horned runt, and when you charge they charge with you, a wall of horns behind the king's, the ground thundering under all of it, and yours hit first and hardest of all, the line in front of you not so much broken as erased.
[Trait: Minotaur King]
