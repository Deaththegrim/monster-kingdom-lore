Skills

The skills of Monster Kingdom: what each covers and how hard things are.

absorb

Drawing a dead foe's soul in and holding it: the Aberrant's craft.

Covers: drawing a defeated foe's soul in and holding it. Not: killing the foe, which is fight, strike or trick. Easy: take a rat's soul. Medium: hold a goblin's soul through pain. Hard: absorb a soul mid-battle. Very hard: keep a stronger monster's soul from tearing loose. Extremely hard: absorb an elder's soul whole.

Primary Attribute: Soul

barter

Trading goods and ingots, reading a deal, haggling.

Covers: trading goods and ingots, haggling, reading a deal. Not: leading monsters, which is command. Easy: swap meat for salt. Medium: haggle an ingot down. Hard: strike a caravan contract. Very hard: win a monopoly from a town. Extremely hard: trade with a dragon and keep your hoard.

Primary Attribute: Cunning

build

Digging, raising and fortifying dens, halls and walls; working materials.

Covers: digging, raising, fortifying and working materials into rooms, walls and gear. Not: trading the results, which is barter. Easy: dig a sleeping hollow. Medium: raise a palisade. Hard: wall a cave mouth with a gate. Very hard: raise a stone hall. Extremely hard: found a city's walls.

Primary Attribute: Cunning

command

Leading a tribe: rallying, ordering, keeping monsters loyal.

Covers: leading a tribe: rallying, ordering, keeping loyalty. Not: trading, which is barter. Easy: get a kobold to fetch. Medium: hold a warband together. Hard: rally a routed tribe. Very hard: rule a town's chiefs. Extremely hard: bend an elder monster to your banner.

Primary Attribute: Soul

endure

Shrugging off poison, cold, hunger and hard travel.

Covers: toughing out poison, cold, hunger, heat and the road. Not: healing others, which is lore. Easy: walk a day unfed. Medium: shake off a snakebite. Hard: cross the ice wastes. Very hard: survive a cave-in. Extremely hard: outlast a curse.

Primary Attribute: Might

evoke

Racial magic and breath: fire, frost, venom, spores, the powers a body or a kept soul carries.

Covers: racial magic, breaths, spores, venoms and aura powers. Not: pulling a soul in, which is absorb. Easy: light a fire with a spark. Medium: scorch a wolf. Hard: freeze a stream to cross it. Very hard: breathe through a war party. Extremely hard: turn a storm on an army.

Primary Attribute: Soul

fight

Fighting by force with tooth, claw, horn, tail or a scavenged weapon: heavy hits, grapples and shoves.

Covers: close combat by force: heavy hits, grappling, shoving, throwing a foe, slugging it out. Not: quick hits and darting in and out, which is strike; feints and dirty tricks, which is trick; racial magic or breath, which is evoke. Easy: drive off a scavenger. Medium: kill a goblin raider. Hard: beat an orc in a ring. Very hard: bring down an ogre. Extremely hard: kill a dungeon floor's keeper.

Primary Attribute: Might

hunt

Tracking, chasing and running prey down.

Covers: tracking, chasing and running prey down. Not: hiding, which is stalk. Easy: follow fresh tracks. Medium: run down a deer. Hard: track a pack across rock. Very hard: find a hidden lair by trail. Extremely hard: hunt a beast that hunts you back.

Primary Attribute: Swiftness

instinct

Monster senses: scent, tremor, danger before it shows.

Covers: monster senses: scent, tremor, danger-sense, reading another monster. Not: knowledge of a race, which is lore. Easy: smell a rival's mark. Medium: feel footsteps through stone. Hard: sense an ambush. Very hard: read a beast's intent. Extremely hard: feel the dungeon itself turn its eye on you.

Primary Attribute: Soul

lore

Knowing races, ruins, the dungeon's depths and the old soul-stories.

Covers: knowledge of races, ruins, the dungeon and soul-stories; tending wounds. Not: monster senses, which is instinct. Easy: name a common beast. Medium: know a race's weakness. Hard: read a ruin's runes. Very hard: recall a lost depth's dangers. Extremely hard: know what the Great Dungeon is.

Primary Attribute: Cunning

stalk

Moving unseen and unheard; ambush.

Covers: moving unseen, ambush and escape. Not: tracking, which is hunt. Easy: creep past a sleeper. Medium: ambush a lone scout. Hard: slip through a camp. Very hard: pass a watchtower by day. Extremely hard: walk unseen through a dragon's hall.

Primary Attribute: Swiftness

strike

Fighting fast: quick hits, darting in and out, slashing past, and thrown weapons.

Covers: fast close combat and thrown weapons: quick hits, darting in and out, slashing past a guard. Not: slugging it out or grappling, which is fight. Easy: nip a scavenger and get clear. Medium: cut a goblin raider down before he swings. Hard: slash past an orc's guard. Very hard: run rings round an ogre and bleed it. Extremely hard: outpace a dungeon floor's keeper hit for hit.

Primary Attribute: Swiftness

talk

Talking your way in or out: persuading, lying, threatening, getting someone to spill.

Covers: persuading, lying, threatening and getting someone to talk. Not: haggling a price, which is barter; giving your own tribe orders, which is command. Easy: talk a guard into looking away. Medium: lie to a goblin boss and walk out. Hard: scare a caravan into paying up. Very hard: talk a warband out of a fight. Extremely hard: lie to a dragon's face and live.

Primary Attribute: Cunning

trick

Fighting dirty: feints, bluffs, dirt in the eyes, and turning the ground and the foe's own weight against them.

Covers: fighting dirty: feints, bluffs mid-fight, blinding, tripping, turning the ground and the foe's weight against them. Not: hitting from hiding, which is stalk. Easy: kick dirt in a scavenger's eyes. Medium: feint a goblin raider into a miss. Hard: trip an orc into the fire. Very hard: lure an ogre off a ledge. Extremely hard: turn a dungeon floor's own traps on its keeper.

Primary Attribute: Cunning
