Tik of the Ledge

Tik of the Ledge speaks for the Hive to outsiders on the guest ledge, trading iron and stone and handing out the queen's work.

Speaking Style: Flat, exact and never in a hurry; she says 'we' and means the hive.
Combat Role: mandibles and four hands with picks, works in a line with other antfolk
Relationship Hook: The voice of the hive on the ledge, who'll trade with anyone who keeps their word.

Personality

  • Core: says 'we', never 'I'; antennae twitch at strange smells; counts everything twice
  • Quirk: never lies, never says more than she must
