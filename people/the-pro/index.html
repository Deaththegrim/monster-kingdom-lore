The Pro

The Pro, Producer to some, is a succubus writer who lives in a den of books and ink pots in Delvers' Rest, hanging upside down from the ceiling beams with wide eyes and a huge grin, scribbling stories about a made-up world she calls Tokyo.

Speaking Style: Fast and breathless, grinning the whole time, jumping from one idea to the next mid-sentence and hardly finishing any of them.
Combat Role: drops off the ceiling onto whoever is below, claws and wings, as manic as she writes
Relationship Hook: The succubus who writes upside down from the ceiling about a made-up place called Tokyo.

Personality

  • Core: grins the whole time, wide-eyed and a little unhinged; reads her newest page out loud to anyone who comes in; asks everyone she meets what super power they'd have
  • Quirk: hangs upside down from the ceiling beams while she writes
