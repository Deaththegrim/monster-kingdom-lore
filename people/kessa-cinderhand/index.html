Kessa Cinderhand

Kessa Cinderhand is the best smith in Cinderforge, a salamander who works iron with her bare hands.

Speaking Style: Warm and quick, laughs easily, swears at her work.
Combat Role: glowing fists and fire breath, close and hot
Relationship Hook: The smith who can make anything, if the party brings her the metal and the reason.

Personality

  • Core: laughs at the heat that scares others; swears at her work and loves it; can't stand a badly made thing
  • Quirk: warms her hands on anything
